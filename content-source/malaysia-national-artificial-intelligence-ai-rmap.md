The **National Artificial Intelligence Roadmap (AI-Rmap) for Malaysia 2021-2025** provides a strategic framework to harness AI for national growth. Aligned with the National Policy on Science, Technology, and Innovation (DSTIN) and MyDIGITAL initiatives, the roadmap sets Malaysia's sights on becoming a high-tech nation by 2030. Here's a summary of its key elements:

### Vision and Mission

The AI-Rmap aims to create a sustainable AI ecosystem that increases employment, boosts productivity, and enhances Malaysia's global competitiveness. This vision relies on the **Quadruple Helix partnership** model—government, academia, industry, and society working in unison to foster innovation and AI growth.

### Core Strategies

1. **AI Governance**: Establish robust governance structures, clear data-sharing guidelines, and ethical frameworks. AI-Rmap proposes creating the AI Coordination and Implementation Unit (AI-CIU) to centralize policy and manage AI-related initiatives.

2. **AI R&D Advancement**: Malaysia prioritizes fundamental and applied AI research. Through the AI-Catalyst, R&D will address local needs, promoting collaboration between institutions, companies, and international partners.

3. **Digital Infrastructure**: Escalate access to data centers, cloud services, and 5G technology, ensuring AI readiness. The roadmap emphasizes Jendela, Malaysia's digital agenda, to provide high-quality broadband coverage.

4. **Fostering AI Talent**: Develop a strong AI workforce through specialized programs and scholarships in collaboration with educational institutions. AI-Rmap targets increased postgraduate research in AI-related fields and a structured talent pipeline.

5. **National AI Innovation Ecosystem**: Establish an AI-Catalyst innovation hub to drive industry-specific AI adoption, increase investment, and showcase Malaysia's AI capabilities globally.

### AI Use Cases

The roadmap highlights **AI applications in key areas** such as smart cities, healthcare, agriculture, and public service enhancement. The integration of AI in these sectors aims to improve quality of life, promote economic growth, and ensure technological relevance in the Fourth Industrial Revolution.

### Conclusion

Malaysia's AI-Rmap is a dynamic, "living" document designed to evolve as technology advances. Its strategic focus on governance, talent, infrastructure, and innovation places Malaysia on a path to becoming an AI leader in Southeast Asia, with a sustainable framework for responsible and inclusive AI development.
