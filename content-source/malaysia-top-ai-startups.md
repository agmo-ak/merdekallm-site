**Top AI Startups in Malaysia: Leading the Way in Innovation and Technology**

Malaysia is home to a growing number of AI startups, each pushing the boundaries of technology and innovation. These companies are developing cutting-edge solutions that address industry-specific needs and support Malaysia's digital transformation goals. From language models and facial recognition to data analytics and digital solutions, here's a look at some of the top AI startups in Malaysia making a significant impact across various sectors.

### 1. **Merdeka LLM**

   Merdeka LLM is providing the first AI Sovereignty As a Service in Malaysia, empowering government and enterprises to have independent AI infrastructure, AI talents, AI model and AI deployments via LLM Infra as a Service, LLM Training as a Service, LLM Foundational Model as a Service. Merdeka LLM has finetuned the very first legal LLM and HR LLM based on pre-trained LLAMA 3.2 3b models. Merdeka LLM also empowers deployment via conversational chatbot, voicebot and digital avatar.

### 2. **Wise AI**

   Specializing in facial recognition and digital identity verification, Wise AI has made its mark as a leading provider of AI solutions for the financial services industry. The company's biometric authentication system supports KYC (Know Your Customer) processes, enabling secure, real-time customer verification. Wise AI's technology is widely adopted by banks, insurance companies, and telecoms in Malaysia, helping to streamline customer onboarding and reduce fraud. By using AI to enhance identity verification, Wise AI is shaping the future of secure digital interactions.

### 3. **Innov8tif**

   Innov8tif is a prominent player in the fields of digital identity, workflow automation, and process digitization. Known for its e-KYC (electronic Know Your Customer) and workflow solutions, Innov8tif helps businesses improve efficiency and reduce operational costs. Its AI-based identity verification technology is widely used in banking, telecommunications, and e-commerce, enabling secure and streamlined customer onboarding processes. By automating identity verification, Innov8tif is making digital interactions safer and more accessible across Malaysia.

### 4. **Dattel**

   Dattel is an AI-driven data analytics startup focused on consumer insights for Southeast Asian markets. Leveraging AI and big data, Dattel gathers and analyzes consumer behavior, allowing businesses to make data-driven decisions. Dattel's platform offers insights into consumer preferences, purchasing patterns, and market trends, making it valuable for companies in retail, finance, and FMCG (fast-moving consumer goods). Their data-centric approach empowers businesses to understand and anticipate consumer needs, giving them a competitive edge in a rapidly evolving market.

### 5. **Kotak Sakti**

   Kotak Sakti combines AI with IoT (Internet of Things) to provide real-time data solutions for logistics, agriculture, and environmental monitoring. Their AI-powered platform collects and processes data from IoT devices, enabling companies to optimize operations, monitor environmental impact, and make informed decisions. In agriculture, for example, Kotak Sakti's sensors and AI analytics can track soil conditions, weather patterns, and crop health, improving yields and sustainability. This startup is instrumental in advancing Malaysia's smart agriculture and logistics sectors.

### 6. **Pixlr**

   Pixlr is a well-known AI-powered photo editing platform that offers a wide range of tools for digital creatives. Originally founded in Sweden, Pixlr now has a strong presence in Malaysia, where its development team continues to innovate with AI-based editing features like background removal, image enhancement, and retouching. The platform caters to everyone from casual users to professional designers, making advanced photo editing accessible and affordable. Pixlr's use of AI enables users to create high-quality visuals quickly, contributing to the booming digital content industry.

### 7. **Tess Innovation**

   Tess Innovation is a health-tech startup specializing in AI-driven healthcare solutions. The company's focus is on developing predictive analytics for healthcare providers, allowing early diagnosis and personalized treatment plans. Tess Innovation's platform leverages AI to analyze medical data and identify trends, providing valuable insights for doctors and healthcare institutions. By enabling data-driven care, Tess Innovation helps reduce costs, improve patient outcomes, and optimize healthcare delivery, aligning with Malaysia's goals for an improved healthcare system.

### 8. **Data Micron**

   Data Micron is an AI and data solutions company known for its expertise in predictive analytics and machine learning. Serving industries such as finance, telecommunications, and manufacturing, Data Micron helps businesses leverage data to make smarter, faster decisions. Their AI-powered tools enable companies to analyze large datasets, forecast trends, and detect anomalies. Data Micron's solutions support operational efficiency, fraud detection, and customer insights, making it a key player in Malaysia's data-driven business landscape.

### 9. **TimeTec**

   TimeTec offers smart city and property management solutions powered by AI and IoT. Their solutions include visitor management systems, security surveillance, workforce management, and smart parking. TimeTec's AI-driven systems are widely used in residential and commercial properties to enhance security, improve operational efficiency, and offer convenient services to residents and tenants. As Malaysia's urban centers grow, TimeTec's innovations contribute to building safer, more connected smart cities that improve quality of life.

### Conclusion

Malaysia's AI startup ecosystem is thriving, with companies across diverse sectors contributing to the nation's digital economy and technological advancement. From digital identity and consumer insights to healthcare and smart cities, these startups are shaping Malaysia's future as a technology-driven economy. With government support, a growing talent pool, and a commitment to innovation, Malaysia is set to become a significant player in AI within Southeast Asia. As these startups continue to expand their capabilities and reach, they will play a crucial role in driving Malaysia's AI transformation forward, benefiting industries, businesses, and communities alike.
